# Sign-in failed

A sign-in through the account platform that does not complete lands here, signed out, with a link that
starts it again toward the same page. The message tells whether to retry at once or in a few minutes.

## Rejected

The account platform's answer did not match the sign-in the platform started, or its code expired.
The visitor may retry at once.
